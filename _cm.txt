Fix SSH login failure: register nologin shell in /etc/shells

SSH/WS tunneling accounts were created with /sbin/nologin, but that shell is
not listed in /etc/shells on Rocky Linux 9. PAM's pam_shells then rejects the
login, surfacing to clients as "incorrect username or password" even with
correct credentials.

- common.sh: add ensure_nologin_shell() that resolves /usr/sbin/nologin
  (EL9 path) and registers it (and /sbin/nologin) in /etc/shells.
- account.sh, ssh/trial-ssh, ssh/recovery-ssh, api/trial-ssh: use the
  resolved nologin path instead of hardcoded /sbin/nologin.
- install.sh: register /usr/sbin/nologin, /sbin/nologin, /bin/false in
  /etc/shells during setup, before any account creation.
